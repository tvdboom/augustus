# Sixteen-frame military idle animations

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the previous eight-frame character sheets. All eleven transparent four-column by four-row sheets live in `assets/images/military/idle/`. Original combat/movement atlases and panel icons are retained. `build.rs` normalizes idle sheets to 768×768 (sixteen 192px cells), using a shared scale and aligning the ground silhouette so moving equipment does not recenter the soldier.

Light infantry cells retain the connected soldier before measuring their bounds. The third source row includes stray spear tips from the next row; removing those fragments prevents a false foot baseline and the resulting jump in apparent height.

Standing playback has a 6.66-second base cycle, with 1.6/0.7/1.4/0.8-second pauses at settled poses and 180ms transitions. Per-army/type phase and pace vary, avoiding synchronized nodding. Feet remain planted while glances, shoulder/torso changes, arm adjustments and species-specific secondary motion provide a relaxed idle.

Army display anchors are geographic and retained until the corresponding stationary force or battle side disappears. Zoom, panning, viewport clipping and changing landmarks do not trigger replacement placement. Province labels/resources are laid out independently. New stationary armies first try to clear the whole centered name/resource group, preferring an offset above it; if no clear ground fits, overlap is allowed. Armies are painted last, above the text.
